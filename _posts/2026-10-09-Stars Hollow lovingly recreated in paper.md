**Paper artist and evident Gilmore Girls fanatic Bruna Salvador Conforto** has lovingly recreated Stars Hollow buildings like Lorelai's house (above), Kim's Antiques, the Black White & Read bookstore and the town square church (bells thankfully disabled).

Check out Bruna's other amazing creations at his [DeviantArt](https://www.deviantart.com/brunasc/gallery/34423165/lorelai%27s-house-model---gilmore-girls) site, where you can marvel at other WB set pieces, as featured in The Mentalist and Pretty Little Liars – and the Friends apartment.  

**All models were made only with paper, then hand-painted.**  Oh if only I had the time.

It's truly astonishing work.
