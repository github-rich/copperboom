In case you were ever in doubt about whether you were more Team Dean than Team Logan, this quick quiz over at Buzzfeed should put your mind at ease.

[Buzzfeed Quiz : Which Gilmore Girls guy is your Soulmate?](http://www.buzzfeed.com/mackenziekruvant/which-gilmore-girls-guy-is-your-soulmate)

**I got Jess.  How about you?**
