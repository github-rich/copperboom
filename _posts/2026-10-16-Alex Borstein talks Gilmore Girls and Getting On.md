Kicking off this year is a terrific interview from the always classy Alex Borstein. 

**She was the originally intended Sookie St James**, played both Drella the harpist in season one and later on, the fabulous Miss Celine (Emily's name-dropping stylist).

In real life she's married to Jackson the produce guy, is the voice of Lois in _Family Guy_ (for which Dan Palladino continues to be one of the showrunners) and is a very funny lady and writer.

> __Alex__  I wouldn't have been that comfortable with Jackson and I - they had them getting married and having kids. I don't think I would have wanted to do that on screen with my real-life husband.

**She spills the beans** on the shenanigans between Fox and MadTV that led to Melissa McCarthy's casting in her place, how Family Guy led to her husband Jackson Douglas playing Jackson Belleville in Gilmore Girls and talks about her current HBO series _Getting On_.
