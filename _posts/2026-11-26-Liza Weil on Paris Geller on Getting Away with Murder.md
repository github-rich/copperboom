[Buzzfeed has a joyous interview](http://www.buzzfeed.com/emilyorley/liza-weil-how-to-get-away-with-murder#3ql3qt5) with Liza Weil reflecting on her role as Paris Geller and her role in _How To Get Away With Murder_.

Liza remarks on how different she is from Paris in real life but also how sometimes, the Geller raises her head.

> __Liza__  If I'm having a particularly challenging day, I'll hear myself and it sounds like Paris and it's scary!

She said yes to Amy Sherman-Palladino's _Bunheads_ without looking at a script.

> __Liza__  I will, always, whenever Amy comes knocking, answer.

**Well, wouldn't we all?**

She goes on to discuss her roles on _Grey's Anatomy_ and _Private Practice_, then the preparation required for her current recurring role as Bonnie Winterbottom in _How To Get Away With Murder_, the Shonda Rhimes legal show.

> __Liza__  I've always played people who are considerably younger, so it's nice to be playing a grown-up.

Liza was already twenty-three when Gilmore Girls started airing, portraying Paris aged sixteen or seventeen.

I can't see Paris ever truly growing up but Liza sure seems to be hitting her stride. As Autumn draws in, it warms me to see the Gilmore alumni doing so well. Lauren Graham (Lorelai), Matt Czuchry (Logan), Jared Padalecki (Dean) and now Liza Weil have great roles in high-end shows. Milo Ventimiglia (Jess) has _The Whispers_ coming up, as well as voicing Spiderman Noir on _Ultimate Spiderman_. Sean Gunn is giving great green screen in the movies and of course Melissa McCarthy is burning so very brightly.

**Are you enjoying Parenthood, The Good Wife, Supernatural or How To Get Away With Murder?**  
**Who do you would you like to see more of?**  
**Do you think Paris would ever change – and would you really want her to?**
